# DataVista Studio — GitHub publishing policy

**Scope:** this policy governs public portfolio content and collaboration inquiries for `DataVistaStudio/DataVistaStudio`, not releases of private engineering repositories.

## Brand and ownership

- **Company:** [DataVistaStudio](https://github.com/DataVistaStudio), the official home for DataVista public presentation and approved independent releases.
- **Founder:** [Mohammad Arslan](https://github.com/arslanhq), the professional founder profile.
- Keep the company and personal profiles distinct. A project must not be moved, renamed, opened, archived or deleted merely to improve appearance.

## What may be shown publicly

- Approved product names, descriptions, public web links and launch/preview status.
- Editorial project artwork approved for public display.
- Public-facing workflows, bounded case studies, properly licensed original material and explicitly cleared open-source work.

## What must remain private

- Credentials, tokens, keys, private access links, raw production configuration and backups.
- Proprietary code, internal QA evidence, security gates, signing or credential custody procedures.
- Personal profiles or contact records not approved for public use, client information, unreleased media feeds, copyrighted material and datasets without appropriate rights.

## Presentation standards

- Artwork and photography must represent the subject accurately. **Editorial illustrations are not screenshots** and must never be described as proof of software features.
- **NEWSROOM** is the newsroom/source-monitoring product.
- **Market.961.MENU** is the current public marketplace project label. Its public site is presently a preparation/preview presence; do not imply a fully launched seller service.
- For the portfolio, retain truthful status labels: public, limited/restricted, preview, under preparation, concept.
- Use human-readable captions, meaningful alt text, Arabic/English clarity, consistent sizing and working links.
- Do not fabricate customer numbers, success metrics, third-party endorsements, live feeds, credentials or approval statuses.

## Verification before updating public materials

1. Confirm the repository's **stable owner identity**, not the visible username alone.
2. Check public product links and current product name.
3. Check artwork rights and suitability; avoid accidental exposure of operator interfaces.
4. Preserve accepted work and separate portfolio changes from application deployments.
5. Verify the change in both public company and founder profiles when cross-linked.

## Release and review boundaries

Repository status, tests and pull requests are separate from deployment acceptance. **Draft, review-only and HOLD items must not be bulk-merged or closed** without the owning project's evidence and approval. Keep independent security and legal gates intact.
