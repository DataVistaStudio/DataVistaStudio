## How a collaboration begins

A useful first inquiry includes:
1. The goal, audience and expected workflow.
2. Countries, languages and platforms involved.
3. Whether the data or media is your own, licensed, public, or otherwise restricted.
4. Whether you need a product demonstration, implementation, integration, or a separately scoped licensing discussion.

Use the **[official DataVista contact page](https://datavista.studio/contact)** for a first conversation. Do **not** post budgets, credentials, customer records, private invitations, or vulnerability details in a public GitHub issue.

## Rights, readiness and delivery

- Third-party news, book, video, imagery and dataset rights must be verified separately; public availability alone does not convey reuse or commercial licensing rights.
- DataVista commercial source code, internal research, production infrastructure and client records are not automatically included in any proposed collaboration.
- Product demonstrations and scope discussions are distinct from security sign-off, production release, service-level commitments or accepted delivery.
- Project access varies: some products have public interfaces, while others are previews or operator-restricted.
